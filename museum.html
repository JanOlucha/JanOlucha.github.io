Percolation Theory

This simulation demonstrates percolation on a grid. The percolation threshold is around \( p_c \approx 0.5927 \) for the square lattice. As \( p \) increases past \( p_c \), a giant component emerges.

Random Walk on a Lattice

A simple symmetric random walk on \( \mathbb{Z}^2 \) where the walker moves to one of the four neighboring sites with equal probability. This is a Markov process.
