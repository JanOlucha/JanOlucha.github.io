Jan Olucha Fuentes

Hi, I'm Jan. I'm a second year PhD student under the supervision of Alex Cox at the University of Bath, working as a part of the MaThRad research programme through the SAMBa Center for Doctoral Training. I did my Master's at the University of Cambridge and my Bachelor's at King's College London.

Mathematical Interests

My mathematical interests are mainly in Probability. Some keywords of my interests: Monte Carlo simulation, variance reduction algorithms, rare-event simulation, Feynman–Kac measures, etc.

Currently, I am interested in understanding the theory behind algorithms for computing expectations of the form \[ \mathbf{E}\left[ f(X_t)\prod_{s=1}^{t} G_s(X_s) \right], \] where \((X_t)_{t\geq 0}\) is a discrete-time Markov chain on a measurable state space \((E,\mathcal{E})\), \(f:E\to\mathbf{R}\) is a test function, and \((G_t)_{t\geq 0}\) is a collection of functions on \(E\), called potential functions.

  • Since 2023 I work with the charity We Solve Problems to bring high quality mathematical enrichment activities to kids. At We Solve Problems we run "Maths Circles", a kind of social club for maths-loving bright young students. Since the summer of 2025, I serve as the Head of Curriculum of this organisation.
