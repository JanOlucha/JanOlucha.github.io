Jan Olucha Fuentes

Hi, I'm Jan. I did my undergraduate degree in Mathematics between 2021 and 2024 at King's College London (KCL), where I was the President of the Mathematics Student Society during my last year. I am currently studying Part III of the Mathematical Tripos at Cambridge, and I will (hopefully) be starting a PhD at the University of Bath under the supervision of Alex Cox in September 2025. This is my CV in the form of a website, where you can find my academic trajectory and some of the stuff I've written.

Mathematical Interests

My mathematical interests are mainly in probability. I enjoy thinking about probabilistic models with physical relevance: interacting particle systems, statistical mechanics, random walks, etc.
